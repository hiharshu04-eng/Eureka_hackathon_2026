Common Queries

Is this open to beginners? +
Yes. First-year and second-year students from all branches are welcome to build and learn.
What is the allowed team composition? +
Teams can have 2 to 4 students. You can also apply solo and find teammates during the briefing session.
Are external UI libraries permitted? +
Submissions are evaluated on clean implementation and originality. For this round, stick to standard web tech.
